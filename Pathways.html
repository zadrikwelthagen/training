What’s next after school? 🤔

Not sure what to do after school? You’re not alone. This interactive tool helps you explore different options, figure out what suits you best, and build your own direction step by step — no pressure, no right or wrong answers.

🚀 How this works

  1. 👉 Pick any pathway that catches your interest
  2. 💬 Answer 5 quick questions (just go with your gut)
  3. 🏅 Save your score and collect a badge
  4. 🔓 Complete all pathways to unlock your Top 3 pathways
  5. 🎯 Use the Final Decision questionare to choose your strongest path
  6. 📝 Generate a full report of your top pathway
  • ✅ There are no wrong answers — just be honest
  • 🔁 You can redo any pathway if you change your mind
  • 🏅 Your progress stays saved as you go
  • ♻️ You can reset everything anytime and start fresh
  • 💡 The more pathways you complete, the clearer your results become
Your future isn’t one single path — this just helps you explore your options and find what fits YOU.
